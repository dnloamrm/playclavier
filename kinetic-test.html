Reading of the two studies: one input (the variable parameter, 0–10) feeds two different curves. The magenta skew stays at zero until a start point, then rises fast and is capped inside the "Goldilocks zone" (never a back slant, never "too slanted"). The green vertical stretch is convex: it stays low and then accelerates. Past the burst point the whole word scales up from its centre until it overflows the screen. Move the slider, hit a note, sweep, or type to drive it with a small built-in synth (the real piano is not loaded on this page).
